Tenex’s groundbreaking solutions will be revealed at the event, held from November 11-14, 2024, in Riyadh. Tenex continues to solidify its place as the go-to tech partner for the GCC’s largest real estate companies and beyond, and this recognition comes as a testament to its innovative approach.
